Our Journey

The spark behind Make A Change Youth

Whenever I ask any teen, "Hey, what have you planned or what is your goal to change?" but every time I always hear, "I'll figure it out when I'm 21." So I asked myself, "Can we only be adults to even think about making a change in our society?" That was my awakening to start this initiative to empower teenagers to transform their visionary ideas into impactful projects. By sharing diverse youth voices from around the world and cultivating a supportive community of changemakers, we are providing the platform for the next generation to lead today and not tomorrow.

M.A.C.Y. was born from the conviction that young people shouldn't have to wait for permission to be heard. We decided to bridge that gap, providing the platform, resources, and community that every young changemaker needs to act now and lead with confidence.
